# Submersibles

## What this is

The submarine protocol (nfx's brief, 2026-10-07; Rusty: "it's probably an extension of vanilla
wheels. It's a new vehicle"). It is layered on Vanilla Wheels 1.13.0 (its D-0030, D-0031) and owns:
- the diving;
- the crew's air;
- the fit-out that takes Immersive Aircraft's upgrades;
- the Torpedo Tube.

Submarine mods (`minecraft-explorer-sub`, `minecraft-scout-sub`) are data. The brief, as received,
is `knowledge/sources/nfx-handoff-2026-10-07.md`; the approved plan is
`~/.claude/plans/peppy-scribbling-lollipop.md`. Decisions: D-0001 (diving), D-0002 (the fit-out),
D-0003 (torpedoes).

## Status: 1.0.0 released 2026-10-08 in pack 1.78.0

- **Released** on Rusty's go ("Release the submarine batch"), with Vanilla Wheels 1.13.0, the Explorer

The status as built:

- **Built (2026-10-07):** the dive, crew air, the wreck that comes up, setting down on water, the
  fit-out (upgrades by Immersive Aircraft's files and rule, tubes, paint, the readout), the
  instruments, torpedoes, the renderer, the box submarine.
- **Gate:**
  - 28 JUnit and 21 gametests, all green (the 21st D-0004's: a broken submarine set down stays to be repaired).
  - Every rule was run against its mutation and caught it: holding depth, rising, floating at the
    draft, beached, durability, air, the wreck, the fuel rate, the reload, creative, setting down,
    the slots, packing, breaking no block, the hull, the hull axis by axis, the crash.
- **Caught by those mutations, not by a first green run:**
  - Floating at the draft and beached went unnoticed at first: two tests happened to end near the
    draft, and a beached hull never moved anyway.
  - Behind the beached one was a real fault. Cutting the move as a whole held a hull pressed into
    a floor fast, so a submarine on the seabed with its planes down could not go ahead. Vanilla
    Wheels 1.13.0 gained `hullClampAxes` for it.
- **The booth** (15 checks) films the box submarine: its draft, a real Left Shift diving it,
  depth held, air, the lamps at night, first person, a torpedo, R at the hatch, the fit-out's
  readout, the hold.
- **The launch's sound** is a CC0 whoosh (D-0003, `devtools/art/sounds/SOURCES.md`), no longer the
  trident's.
- **The submarines** are built and verified in their own repos: `minecraft-explorer-sub` (four
  seats, 8 gametests, its booth's 17 checks) and `minecraft-scout-sub` (one seat, 8 gametests, 16
  checks). Their ports and the Scout's bubble are clear from inside through Vanilla Wheels' cockpit

- **Rusty passed the photos** ("Looks good proceed") and drove the subs in a 4070 playtest (Prism
  instance `submarine-playtest`, world "Submarine Harbour"), which brought Vanilla Wheels' D-0033

  feel numbers unchanged.

## Shape

- `domain`: `Dive`, `Hullform`, `DiveInput`, `Fitting`, `Upgrades`, `TorpedoRun`.
- `main`: `Submarine` extends Vanilla Wheels' `Vehicle` and overrides its hooks; `Torpedo`;
  `UpgradeTable`; `FitOutMenu`; `Launching`; `api` (`submersibles:submarine`, the tags); `net`
  (controls, fire); `client`.
- `gametest`: the box submarine (generated by `devtools/art/build.py`), stand-ins for Immersive
  Aircraft's upgrades on vanilla items in its file format, and the template `tank` (48 by 24 by 24,
  walled with barriers), filled by each test.
