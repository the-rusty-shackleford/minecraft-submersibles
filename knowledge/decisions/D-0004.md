# D-0004 — A broken submarine can be set down and mended

Rotorcraft's D-0004, the same fault and the same fix. A wreck under water comes up before it is
destroyed (D-0001); that was done every tick for any submarine at no condition that was not wholly
under water, so a broken submarine set down on the water from its item was packed again on its first
tick, and could never be repaired.

**Decision**: only a wreck made under water is packed when it comes up (`Submarine.wreckComingUp`,
set when the destroy is deferred, saved). A broken submarine set down from its item floats where it
is put, unpowered (buoyant: D-0001), until it is repaired by hand or on the lift; hurt again, it is
packed again.

**Verification**: the GameTest `aBrokenSubmarineSetDownOnWaterStaysToBeRepaired` fails on the old
rule (the submarine gone); `aWreckAtDepthComesUpWithItsRiderBeforeItIsPacked` still passes.

**Links**: `Submarine.destroy`, `Submarine.tick`; D-0001; Rotorcraft's D-0004.
