# D-0003 — Torpedoes

nfx's brief asked for subs that "can be fitted with guns", and settled that guns must work at
depth: Immersive Aircraft's cannon dies in about 19 blocks under water, and its crossbow and bomb
bay are useless down there. Its weapons are built around its own vehicle class
(`immersive_aircraft.entity.VehicleEntity`) and cannot mount on a Vanilla Wheels vehicle at all.

**Decision**:

- **The Torpedo Tube** is an item that fits a weapon slot. Each weapon slot is a muzzle the
  profile names (`weapons`, mesh units), fixed forward: you aim by pointing the submarine.
- **Use** (right mouse, Immersive Aircraft's weapon key) fires from the pilot's seat while a tube
  is fitted, and then uses nothing in hand. The tubes fire in turn, each reloading for 2 s.
- **Ammunition:** a torpedo comes from the hold, one a shot. A creative pilot takes none, as
  creative spends nothing consumable. An empty hold says so above the hotbar.
- **The run** (`domain/TorpedoRun`): its own motor holds it at 1.2 blocks a tick in water, the way
  it was going, for 96 blocks; spent, it sinks harmlessly. Out of the water it falls and slows as
  a thrown thing does. It is never pushed by a current, trails bubbles in water and smoke in air.
- **On contact** with a block or anything alive it explodes with a creeper's power (3), hurting
  what is near and wearing vehicles, and **breaks no block**. Under water an explosion breaks
  nothing anyway; at the waterline it would only cost docks and piers.
- **It never strikes** the submarine that fired it, or anyone aboard.
- **Recipes:**
  - the tube: three iron above, a dispenser, redstone and a piston, three iron below;
  - two torpedoes: TNT, an iron and a copper ingot, gunpowder.
- **The launch's sound** is an underwater whoosh cut from a CC0 recording (csaszi's designed
  torpedo pass-bys, freesound 336469; `devtools/art/sounds/SOURCES.md`): one pass-by from 0.3 s
  before its peak, so it starts hard and goes away, at a pitch drawn from 0.92 to 1.08. Until the
  submarines' own art it was the trident's riptide.

**Why**:
- A torpedo is the one weapon that works at depth.
- Blocks are spared because a submarine's targets are mobs, boats and other vehicles. Rusty found
  guns that broke blocks "too destructive" (Ranged Weapons' D-0011).

**Alternatives rejected**:
- *Immersive Aircraft's weapons*: they cannot mount here.
- *Ranged Weapons Mod guns on a mount*: not in v1. That is a feature of their protocol, and its
  bullets' water behaviour is unmeasured.
- *Rotating mounts*: a fixed tube is a submarine's.

**Verification**: `TorpedoRunTest` (3); `TorpedoGameTests` (4):
- a torpedo crosses the tank under water and kills a zombie 34 blocks off, glass beside it
  standing, one torpedo gone from the hold, the submarine untouched;
- out of the water one strikes glass three blocks off and the glass stands (the rule tried where
  it matters: under water the water's resistance would hide a breach);
- an empty hold fires nothing and a creative pilot fires all the same;
- two tubes fire in turn, a third press finds them reloading, and after the reload a tube fires
  again.

The rule that a torpedo never strikes its own submarine is a safeguard no test drives: the muzzle
is ahead of the hull and the torpedo outruns it.
