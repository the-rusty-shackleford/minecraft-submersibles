# D-0002 — The fit-out: upgrades, weapons, paint

nfx's brief (2026-10-07): "the upgrades and upgrade slots from Man of Many Planes must work in
these subs." Man of Many Planes ships none of its own. Read off its 0.2.1 jar, it is two planes
whose slots, upgrades and screen are Immersive Aircraft's. The eleven upgrade items are Immersive
Aircraft 1.4.6's, and each is plain data: `data/<ns>/aircraft_upgrades/<item>.json`, an object of
stat name to delta. Both mods are in our pack. A Vanilla Wheels vehicle has no upgrade slots.

**Decision**:

- **The fit-out.** A crouching click on the hull that nothing else claims opens it: a chest, a
  door and the radio keep their own clicks. It holds:
  - a row of upgrade slots, as many as the profile's `upgrades`;
  - a weapon slot for each of the profile's `weapons` muzzles;
  - a paint slot;
  - and a readout: top speed, fuel burnt and wear taken, in percent of the bare hull, plus
    "Instruments" when fitted.

  Each slot takes one item:
  - an upgrade slot `#submersibles:upgrades`, which holds `#immersive_aircraft:upgrades`
    (optional) and whatever a data pack adds;
  - a weapon slot `#submersibles:weapons` (the Torpedo Tube);
  - the paint slot a dye, which paints the hull and stays in the slot as Immersive Aircraft's dye
    slot does. The lift that paints is on land.

  The fit-out goes into the packed item and comes back out of it. If the hull is destroyed
  without packing, it drops.
- **What an upgrade does** is read, as that mod reads it, from the same `aircraft_upgrades` files
  in every data pack, on the server (`UpgradeTable`, a reload listener; the top pack's file wins
  as for any data). Nothing links to Immersive Aircraft. Without it there are no such files and
  no upgrades. A file that is not an object of numbers is refused, named in the log.
- **Its rule** (`domain/Fitting`, read from its bytecode,
  `InventoryVehicleEntity.getTotalUpgrade`). For each stat the total starts at 1; each negative
  delta multiplies it by `1 + delta`; then each positive delta is added; it is floored at 0. A stat
  is its base times its total, except the stabilizer, which that mod adds:
  `base + total - 1`.
- **The stats** are the profile's `properties`, by that mod's names, so an upgrade in its format
  applies by name: `engineSpeed`, `friction`, `verticalSpeed`, `yawSpeed`, `acceleration` (the
  propeller's spool), `fuel` (Vanilla Wheels' fuel rate), `durability` (wear is divided by it),
  `stabilizer` and `wind`. The upgrades that name `dials` or the `hud` carry instruments: depth,
  heading and speed at the top left while aboard.
- **The drag** is `friction` plus the hull's own drag, `friction` a third of it. The Enhanced
  Propeller (friction × 0.25) then gives about a third more speed, not four times.
- **The fitted totals** are computed on the server and synced on the entity, so the pilot's
  client dives by them.

| Upgrade | Its deltas | In a submarine |
|---|---|---|
| Hull Reinforcement | durability +1.0 | half the wear |
| Nether Engine | engineSpeed +0.4, fuel +0.3 | +40% thrust, 30% more fuel |
| Steel Boiler | engineSpeed +0.25, fuel +0.5 | +25% thrust, 50% more fuel |
| Sturdy Pipes | engineSpeed +0.1 | +10% thrust |
| Eco Engine | engineSpeed −0.2, fuel −0.75 | −20% thrust, a quarter of the fuel |
| Industrial Gears | fuel −0.2 | 20% less fuel |
| Enhanced Propeller | friction −0.75 | about a third faster, coasts further |
| Improved Landing Gear | acceleration +0.5 | the propeller spools half again as fast |
| Gyroscope | wind −1.0, stabilizer +0.03 | currents no longer push it; it levels faster |
| Gyroscope (Dials / HUD) | the same + dials / hud | and the instruments |

**Why**: reading the data rather than linking the mod needs nothing of it at run time, works for
any addon in its format, and is the contract it documents for addons. Applying its rule, stat by
stat as it does, makes an upgrade do here what a friend expects from his planes.

**Alternatives rejected**:
- *Linking Immersive Aircraft's registry*: a compile dependency on another mod's internals
  (class names that move between its versions), and a hard one at run time.
- *Our own upgrade items in v1*: Immersive Aircraft would read our files too, and its loader's
  view of a stat it does not know is not ours to rely on.
- *The weapon slots taking Immersive Aircraft's weapons*: they are built around its own vehicle
  class and cannot mount on ours (D-0003).
- *Linear drag on `friction` alone*: the Enhanced Propeller would make a submarine four times as
  fast.

**Verification**:
- JUnit:
  - `FittingTest` (9): Immersive Aircraft 1.4.6's own deltas as fixtures; none; a bonus; a
    penalty; penalty-then-bonus (Eco + Nether = 1.2, not 1.12); two penalties multiply (0.0625);
    the floor; the stabilizer added; instruments; refused numbers.
  - `UpgradesTest` (3): parse; refused files naming themselves; the item a path names.
- `FitOutGameTests` (4), with stand-ins for its upgrades on vanilla items in the test mod (its
  format, its directory):
  - measured under way, a Nether Engine goes 1.4 times as fast and burns 1.3 times the fuel, an
    Eco Engine 0.8 and a quarter;
  - the slots take only their own kind, a shift-click finds the weapon slot, and a dye paints the
    hull;
  - the fit-out survives packing and setting down;
  - a gyroscope with dials carries instruments, adds its stabilizer and stills the current.
- Not yet seen: the real Immersive Aircraft items in the slots (the 4070 playtest, with both mods
  in the instance).
